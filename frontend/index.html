Core Features

Designed for Campus Collaboration

CampusSkillConnect makes learning from your peers structured, reciprocal, and straightforward.

Share Skills

List the academic, technical, or creative skills you have mastered. Help fellow students overcome obstacles while reinforcing your own mastery.

Find Your Match

Discover student partners whose skill offerings match what you need to learn, creating balanced reciprocal learning opportunities across departments.

Learn Together

Propose session dates and times, coordinate meeting locations on campus or online, and manage all session requests in one clean dashboard.

Simple Process

How CampusSkillConnect Works

Getting started takes only three simple steps.

1

Create Profile & List Skills

Sign up with your college email. Add the skills you are confident teaching, along with topics you want to learn this semester.

2

Connect With Peer Matches

Browse compatible students whose needs align with your strengths. Review their academic year, department, and skill details.

3

Schedule & Exchange

Send a session request with your preferred time and topic. Once accepted, meet up to learn, build projects, and grow together.
